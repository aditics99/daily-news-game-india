## Reuse decision

Do not copy or hotlink any of these cartoons to Explain That based on the POLITICO page alone. No open license for the gallery artwork was visible. The Cagle licensing terms require a license for the particular use; Tribune Content Agency provides a reprints/licensing request for online use; Creators Syndicate requires express written permission and electronic rights; Andrews McMeel likewise handles reprint licensing. For credits tied to a newspaper, personal site, or POLITICO, the gallery still does not grant us permission. Ask the relevant artist or rights holder for each cartoon if we want to feature it.

This is a rights-screening decision for our publication workflow, not a legal opinion. We are using POLITICO to study the weekly curation and attribution pattern only. No POLITICO cartoon image or explanatory text is included in the app.
